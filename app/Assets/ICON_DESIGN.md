# App icon

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. Original PNG is retained in AppIcon.png; build.sh creates standard macOS ICNS sizes without changing the artwork. Black/graphite tile, silver photograph and clock communicate photo timezone adjustment. No text, brands or user photos.

## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production macOS app icon for a photo EXIF timezone batch editor. Create one polished minimal black technology aesthetic app icon, square composition. Center a bold silver-white photograph frame with a simple mountain/photo silhouette, with a clearly integrated circular clock at its lower right; a minimal orbit/UTC adjustment cue in the clock ring communicates correcting photo timezones, not image filters. Matte near-black rounded-square tile, subtle graphite edge and understated satin depth, crisp cool-white symbol, monochrome black/graphite/silver palette. Large simple shapes legible at 32px, balanced negative space. The rounded square occupies 88% of canvas, with genuinely transparent corners/exterior. No lettering, no numbers, no watermark, no brands, no screenshot, no device mockup, no extra symbols. Straight-on square icon only, not perspective. High quality 1024x1024 or larger square PNG.
